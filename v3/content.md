projects:
  - id: "SFUDA"
    index: "01"
    kicker: "Project 01 / Fetal Brain Mask Seg"
    title: "Source-Free Unsupervised Domain Adaptation for Fetal Brain MRI"
    short: "Robust fetal brain extraction across unseen centers and MRI sequences."
    image: "/v3/assets/images/project1.jpg"
    image_alt: "FetalSFUDA project figure"
    description: "Fetal brain extraction is essential for motion tracking, reconstruction, and quantitative MRI analysis, yet models trained at one site often fail on images from a new scanner or sequence. This project adapts a pretrained model using only unlabeled target images, without sharing source images or requiring target annotations. It combines predictive entropy minimization with a fetal-brain class-ratio prior and extends the same idea from structural MRI to challenging diffusion-weighted MRI."
    outputs:
      - "ISMRM 2025 - Source-Free Unsupervised Domain Adaptation for Fetal Brain Extraction"
      - "ISMRM Workshop 2025 - Fetal Brain Extraction from Diffusion-weighted MRI"
  - id: "BioTTA"
    index: "02"
    kicker: "Project 02 / Generalizable Biometry"
    title: "BioTTA"
    short: "Source-free test-time adaptation for robust fetal brain biometry across clinical domains."
    image: "/v3/assets/images/project2.jpg"
    image_alt: "BioTTA project figure"
    description: "BioTTA is a source-free, unsupervised test-time adaptation framework for robust automatic fetal brain biometry. A shared encoder and dual decoders jointly localize anatomical landmarks and regress biometric measurements; at inference, entropy, length-consistency, boundary-gradient, and atlas-informed anatomical constraints adapt the model to each unlabeled case. The system quantifies 11 standard fetal brain biometrics across centers, scanners, reconstruction methods, and pathologies, then maps measurements to gestational-age growth trajectories for automated risk stratification and reporting."
    outputs:
      - "MIDL 2025 - Anatomy-guided Test-Time Adaptation for Automated Fetal Brain MRI Morphometry"
      - "ISMRM 2026 - BioTTA: Maximizing Domain Generalization in Automatic Fetal Brain Biometry with Test-Time Adaptation"
      - "OHBM 2026 - Towards Robustness Fetal Brain Development Monitoring and Universal Multi-Disease Diagnosis"
      - "MICCAI 2026 - Maximizing Domain Generalization in Automated Fetal Brain Biometry"
  - id: "FRACE"
    index: "03"
    kicker: "Project 03 / Radiology Impression Generation"
    title: "FRACE"
    short: "Domain-adaptive language modeling with clinical knowledge augmentation for fetal brain MRI reporting."
    image: "/v3/assets/images/project3.jpg"
    image_alt: "FRACE project figure"
    description: "FRACE develops an 8-billion-parameter language-model pipeline for fetal brain MRI diagnostic report generation. It combines parameter-efficient supervised fine-tuning with clinical knowledge-graph retrieval for quantitative and qualitative findings, and uses center-specific domain-adaptive pre-training to improve robustness across institutions. The released adapters work with the DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B base model, while private patient reports remain excluded from the public repository."
    outputs:
      - "PIPPI 2025 Oral - FetalExtract-LLM: Structured Information Extraction in Free-Text Fetal MRI Reports"
